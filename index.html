    How the rating study works

    Scenario inputs, repeatability, and the line between analysis and betting.

    Deterministic browser model

    Calculation

    Each team has a synthetic rating, drift, and dispersion. The browser generates seeded lognormal paths and comparison bands. Head-to-head and bracket panels are deterministic scenario views, not live odds.

    Reproducibility

    The team snapshot is committed with the source. The same team, horizon, path count, and seed produce the same result.

    Limits

    • No fixture, roster, result, bookmaker, or live rating feed is connected.
    • Recognisable team names are labels only; all numerical values are synthetic.
    • No predictive edge, betting backtest, or wagering recommendation is claimed.
    • A real evaluation would require timestamped pre-match ratings and settled outcomes.
